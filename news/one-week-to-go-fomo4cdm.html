One week to go until FOMO4CDM!

October 15, 2026

How can foundation models move from complex clinical data to better clinical decisions?

On 21 October, together with our collaborators, we are organising FOMO4CDM: Foundation Models for Clinical Decision Making, a focused session at BNAIC/BeNeLearn 2026 in Maastricht.

Join us to explore why foundation models matter in clinical care, the opportunities they offer and how they can be applied across different clinical settings.

The session will explore:

  • Representation: Learning from different types of clinical data
  • Clinical reasoning: Supporting diagnosis and treatment decisions
  • Interaction: Enabling human-AI collaboration in clinical workflows
  • Adaptation and evaluation: Tailoring and testing models for clinical settings
  • Responsible AI: Addressing fairness, privacy, safety and regulation
  • Deployment: Bringing models into real clinical practice

The programme will feature invited keynote talks by Maarten De Vos and Francesco Ciompi, contributed research presentations and an interactive panel discussion on trustworthiness and clinical adoption.

We welcome researchers, clinicians, health informaticians, policymakers and students. No previous expertise in foundation models is required.

Interested in the future of clinical AI? Register and join us in Maastricht.
