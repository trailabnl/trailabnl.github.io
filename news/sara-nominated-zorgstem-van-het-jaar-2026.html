Sara nominated for Zorgstem van het Jaar 2026!

October 9, 2026

One of our members, Sara Ben Hmido, has been nominated for Zorgstem van het Jaar 2026!

As a physician-researcher working on AI in healthcare, Sara is passionate about developing technological innovations that truly reflect the needs, experiences and values of patients and healthcare professionals.

Since September 2026, she has also been coordinating initiatives at the Amsterdam Center for AI & Health (AmsCAIH), bringing together AI and healthcare. Alongside her research and work in AI & health, Sara is actively involved as a mentor, teacher and advocate for students, particularly first-generation students.

We are proud to have Sara as a member of TrAIL and would love to see her work and commitment recognised.
