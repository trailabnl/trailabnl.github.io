Dr. Sjors!

November 30, 2024

Dr. In t’ Veld’s work represents a pivotal advancement in the field of liquid biopsy, establishing tumor-educated platelets (TEPs) as a highly versatile and clinically significant biosource for disease detection and monitoring. By leveraging the dynamic RNA profiles of TEPs—cells uniquely shaped by the presence of disease—we present a compelling case for their use in precision diagnostics across a spectrum of cancers and complex disorders. Through the development and validation of high-accuracy machine learning algorithms, this work achieves diagnostic performance that rivals or exceeds current standards, with particular strength in early-stage detection where clinical need is greatest. Our integrated pipeline, spanning optimized RNA extraction protocols to advanced bioinformatic modeling, enables rapid, reproducible, and minimally invasive diagnostics, marking a major step toward scalable clinical implementation.

Crucially, this research extends beyond oncology, demonstrating that TEP RNA signatures can detect and distinguish non-malignant diseases such as multiple sclerosis and pulmonary hypertension, revealing a broader biological utility of platelets as systemic sensors of health and disease. With high specificity, tissue-of-origin classification capabilities, and strong validation across diverse patient cohorts, our findings redefine the role of blood-based biomarkers and set a new benchmark for diagnostic innovation. This work lays the foundation for a new generation of liquid biopsy tools—ones that are not only sensitive and specific, but also practical, rapid, and translatable—positioning TEP RNA analysis as a transformative platform in the era of personalized medicine.

Congratulations, Sjors!
