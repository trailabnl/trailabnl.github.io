PhD vacancy!

March 24, 2025

Within #laboratory medicine we are working hard to improve diagnostics. #Artificial #Intelligence is a means to further improve those diagnostics. Do you want to work as a #PhD candidate on projects in which Artificial Intelligence will be applied in laboratory medicine? Then respond to this #vacancy!

You will work on clinical algorithms and develop risk models with machine learning to optimize the specificity and sensitivity of various tests. The results of the studies you will perform are intended to be implemented. Your results will therefore be applied in patient care. For this research, you will collaborate with various disciplines within the Laboratory Medicine department of the Amsterdam UMC, including clinical chemists, AI specialists and physicians. You will take courses, publish your results in international journals, present at international conferences and complete this research with a dissertation after four years.
