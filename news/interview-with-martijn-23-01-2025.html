Interview with Martijn

January 23, 2025

As a risk and prediction model, as decision support for the physician, as a tool for process optimization, artificial intelligence (AI) is penetrating all facets of healthcare. Does it also lead to better patient care? Prof. Dr. Martijn Schut, professor of Translational AI in Laboratory Medicine at the Amsterdam UMC, and Marleen Vreeburg, ER physician and medical manager ER of the St. Antonius Hospital in Nieuwegein, are convinced of this.
