ACCENT

February 21, 2025

The study of relationships between genes and diseases is a focus area with applications ranging from better understanding the efficacy of drugs or the design of new ones, to the anticipation of disease onset and the eventual acting on preventative actions. This research effort also finds its place in personalized medicine. Because of a lack of strong factual knowledge the relations between genes and diseases are established in a hypothetical way with a range of supporting evidences. Resources like Disgenet, for instance, establish such links (referred to as ”GDAs” – gene-disease associations) via variant shortcuts and text mining. A variant shortcut can be expressed as ”If a variant on gene X is found to often enough found in combination with disease Y, then we assume X and Y to be related.”. Another type of shortcut could be one via a drug: ”if a drug targeting the protein expressed by Gene X is prescribed for the disease Y then X and Y are related”. These two examples of reasoning can be efficiently modeled in a Knowledge Graph made of multi-omics information. We aim to improve on AI-Human collaboration around a knowledge graphs.
