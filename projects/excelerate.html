EXCELERATE

February 21, 2025

A new and disruptive paradigm for building AI systems has emerged in recent years: train a single foundation model on a huge amount of data and adapt it to many applications. The exceptionally rapid adoption of ChatGPT and DALL-E show that this paradigm is very successful. In medicine, such foundation modelling will drastically transform the way that risk models are developed, implemented and interpreted. This proof-of-concept study will build a single foundation model using clinical laboratory data (i.e., full blood count tests) and with transfer learning adapt it to different risk models for infectious diseases and heart diseases.
